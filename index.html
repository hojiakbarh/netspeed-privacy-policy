1. Introduction

NetSpeed ("the App") is a free Android application that monitors internet speed, counts steps, and tracks GPS-based movement speed. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

NetSpeed does not:

  • Collect or store personal information
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Create user accounts
  • Track your identity
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or crash reporting services

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

DataPurposeWhere stored
Daily internet usage (MB)Usage statisticsDevice only
Monthly internet usage (MB)Usage historyDevice only
WiFi usage (MB)WiFi trackingDevice only
Mobile data usage (MB)Mobile trackingDevice only
Step countPedometerDevice only
App settingsPreferencesDevice only

You can delete all data anytime from:
Settings → Data Management → Clear Today's Data

Important: Location data is used only in real-time to calculate movement speed. It is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

5. Advertising

The free version of NetSpeed may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers according to Google's Privacy Policy.

You can manage ad preferences through your device's Google settings.

6. Children's Privacy

NetSpeed does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is rated "Everyone" and contains no inappropriate content.

7. Security

All data is stored using Android's secure SharedPreferences system. No data is transmitted over the internet (except for the speed test feature which connects to public speed test servers).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
